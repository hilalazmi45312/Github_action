Add custom badges to WooCommerce products to highlight promotions.

== Description ==

Boost Your Sales with Eye-Catching Badges!

Enhance your product images and grab customers' attention with our easy-to-use WooCommerce plugin: Product Promotion Badge.

### Key Features:

- **Custom Badges and Frames**: Add unique badges or frames to your product images to highlight promotions, discounts, or special features.
  
- **Flexible Display Conditions**: Choose when and where your badges appear. Set conditions based on:
  - **Date**: Schedule badges to display during sales events or promotional periods.
  - **Product Tags**: Target specific products with tags.
  - **Product Categories**: Apply badges to entire categories for maximum impact.
  - **Stock Status**: Display badges for in-stock or out-of-stock products.
  - **Sale Status**: Highlight products that are currently on sale.
  - **Featured Product Status**: Showcase your featured products with eye-catching badges.

### Why Choose Product Promotion Badge?

- **Increase Visibility**: Make your products stand out with visually appealing badges.
- **Drive Sales**: Highlight special offers and boost your conversion rates.
- **Easy to Use**: User-friendly interface makes it simple to add and manage badges.
- **Customizable**: Tailor badges to fit your brand's style and promotional needs.
